# Mentorae — Smart Student Mentorship & Academic Tracker 🎓

Mentorae is an educational management tool designed to help institutions and educators track student performance, progress, and mentorship needs — combining academic data, behavioral insights, and mentorship feedback in one clean interface.

---

## 📚 What Mentorae Does

- Manage a **database of students** with academic and behavioral metrics.  
- Identify students needing **mentorship or support** early based on performance and behavior trends.  
- Maintain **mentorship logs and feedback** for students.  
- Generate **reports and analytics dashboards** to monitor class-wide performance and at-risk student trends.  
- Export **PDF / printable reports** for academic review or parent-teacher meetings.  

---

## 🎯 Key Features

- ✅ Student profiles with academic and behavioral data.  
- ✅ Data-driven risk / support identification.  
- ✅ Mentorship / teacher feedback logging.  
- ✅ Analytics & dashboards for batch-level insights.  
- ✅ Reports export (PDF or print-friendly).  
- ✅ Flexible: works as standalone internal tool (no login required) or integrated institution-wide system.
